SPECTAL GARDEN
Echofield Reliquary
A Sacred Cathedral Space & Acoustic Portal Simulator

Echofield Reliquary is an ambient reverberation and delay-matrix designed to evoke the warm, dusty,
and slightly eerie atmosphere of a forgotten cathedral. Combining visual aesthetics reminiscent of stained-glass
altars with a modern DSP console, the plugin serves as a creative sound-design tool for generating organic, evolving
spaces and modulated echoes.

1. Consumer Guide (For Music Producers & Musicians)

What is Echofield Reliquary?

Unlike dry, clinical algorithmic reverbs, Echofield Reliquary focuses on character. It emulates sound passing through
aged, reflective stone chambers, crumbling chapels, and spectral corridors. It is best suited for ambient
soundscapes, ethereal vocals, haunting synth pads, and experimental sound design.

The 5 Stained-Glass Shards (Impulse Relics)
The central "Relic Wheel" represents five distinct acoustic spaces (or "relics"). Changing this selection shifts the
character of the physical delay reflections:

1. Bell Tower (Shard I): Highly reflective, metallic, and resonant high-frequency spaces.
2. Choir Hall (Shard II): Dense, warm mid-range reflections designed to flatter vocal frequencies.
3. Stone Vault (Shard III): Massive, heavy, damp, low-frequency-focused stone chambers.
4. Broken Chapel (Shard IV): Unpredictable, diffused, fractured reflections with slight structural anomalies.
5. Spectral Choir (Shard V): Ethereal, highly modulated, pitch-bent, and ghost-like echoes.

Parameters & Controls

The interface features eight radial controls surrounding the relic wheel, arranged for immediate access:

. Size: Controls the physical volume and length of the simulated space.
. Decay: Adjusts the feedback decay time of the reflection loops, ranging from short slaps to infinite sustain.
. Warp: Modulates the internal delay lines, bending the pitch and creating physical "refraction" or chorus-like
movement.
. Diffusion: Controls the reflection density, smoothing out individual delay taps into a wash of sound.
. Tone: Adjusts the spectral dampening of the space, moving from dark, heavy stone to bright, crystalline
structures.
. Pre-Delay: Sets the offset time between the dry signal and the onset of reflections.
. Mix: Blends the dry input and wet space signal (0% to 100%).
. Relic Select: Cycles between the 5 sacred acoustic shards.

Installation & Quick Start
1. Copy the compiled Echofield Reliquary.vst3 file into your system's VST3 directory:
o Windows: C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
2. Open your preferred DAW (FL Studio, Ableton Live, Reaper, Cubase, Pro Tools, etc.) and run a plugin scan.
3. Load the plugin onto an auxiliary send track or directly on a sound source (e.g., a pad or vocal track).
4. Tum up Mix to around 40%, set Size to 70%, and select Choir Hall on the central wheel. Slowly
introduce Warp to add a subtle pitch-shimmer to the reflections.
